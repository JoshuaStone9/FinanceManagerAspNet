# Phase 10.12 — Financial Trends

Adds recorded-data trends for monthly cash flow and passive income.

## Cash flow
- Operating and passive income are separated.
- Core spending combines essential bills, everyday spending and extra expenses.
- Future allocations combine investments and Money Pot contributions.
- Remaining money includes saved monthly carry-forward.

## Passive income
- Uses Income workspace categories: Interest, Dividend(s), Rental income and Other passive income.
- Range controls support 3, 6 and 12 months.
- This phase does not reconstruct historical account balances; balance and net-worth snapshots remain a later phase.
