# Phase 9.1 — Monthly Financial Health Summary

The dashboard now interprets monthly finance data instead of only displaying totals.

## Included

- Overall monthly status: Healthy, Stable, Tight month, or Needs attention.
- Percentage of available income already committed.
- Essential-bills and future-allocation percentages.
- Current surplus or shortfall.
- Comparison with the immediately preceding month when data exists.
- Plain-English insights covering remaining money, essential commitments,
  investments and Money Pots, and changes in everyday spending.
- Responsive and accessible dashboard presentation.

The calculations use the selected dashboard month. Available income includes the
month's recorded income and effective carry forward. No database migration is required.
