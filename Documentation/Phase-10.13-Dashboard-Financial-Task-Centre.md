# Phase 10.13 — Dashboard Financial Task Centre

Finance Reminders has been upgraded into **Financial Tasks**.

## Included

- Dashboard "Today's priorities" area with a direct route to the full task inbox.
- Renamed navigation item with a due-task badge.
- A unified task centre containing manual reminders, automatic Money Pot reminders, and smart prompts.
- Smart prompts for stale account balances, expected interest awaiting confirmation, and Money Pots approaching target dates.
- Priority styling, categories, due dates, deep links, snooze, complete, dismiss, and reopen actions.
- Responsive card and list layouts.

Smart prompts are derived live from finance data and therefore do not need to be completed or dismissed. They disappear automatically once their underlying condition is resolved.
