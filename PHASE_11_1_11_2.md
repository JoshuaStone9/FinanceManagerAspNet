# Phase 11.1–11.2

## Investment account evolution

Account Management now separates four ideas:

- provider, such as Trading 212, Moneybox, Vanguard or a bank;
- account wrapper, such as Savings, Cash ISA, Stocks & Shares ISA or General Investment Account;
- holding type, such as Cash, Money Market Fund, Stocks & Shares or Mixed;
- current recorded balance and the balance the account started with in Finance Manager.

This structure intentionally avoids treating a provider as an investment strategy. A Trading 212 account can therefore hold cash, a money-market fund or stocks and shares.

## Starting balances

Starting balances are intended for existing accounts and Money Pots being brought into Finance Manager.

For an existing record, changing the starting balance changes the current balance only by the same difference. Contributions and withdrawals recorded after the starting point remain represented in the current balance.

Example:

- original starting amount: £1,000;
- current balance after recorded activity: £1,300;
- corrected starting amount: £1,100;
- corrected current balance: £1,400.

This avoids recording imported money as a new monthly contribution.
