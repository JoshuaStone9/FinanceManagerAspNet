# Phase 11.5–11.6 — Intelligent Forecasting and Interest Reconciliation

> This initial record includes the earlier Apply pending interest action. [Phase 11.6.1](phase-11.6.1-interest-reconciliation-workflow.md) replaces that action with Update balance; later handling rules are indexed in the [phase guide](README.md).

## Phase 11.5

The forecast now:

- applies account tax treatment and future tax-effective dates;
- separates gross interest, estimated tax and net interest;
- applies each Money Pot's ordered investment journey stages;
- changes expected growth when a stage begins or ends;
- shows projected investment growth and the journey used for each pot.

## Phase 11.6

Interest entered through the monthly income workspace remains pending against its source account until reconciled.

Account Management now shows:

- recorded balance;
- every unreconciled interest payment accumulated across months;
- suggested balance;
- a one-click Apply pending interest action.

Applying pending interest updates the account balance, marks the linked passive-income records as reconciled and preserves the original monthly income history. The dashboard adds a task while any interest remains unapplied.
