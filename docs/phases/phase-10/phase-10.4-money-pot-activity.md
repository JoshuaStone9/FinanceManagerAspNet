# Phase 10.4 — Money Pot Activity

The legacy Monthly Funding Review has been removed from the application because its planned funding, recovery, shortfall and carried-excess model no longer matches Finance Manager V2.

The replacement Money Pot Activity page is read-only and contribution-driven. It shows:

- total contributions for the selected month;
- contribution count, largest contribution and most-funded pot;
- a dated contribution timeline;
- current balance and optional target for each pot;
- percentage progress and remaining amount;
- estimated completion from actual contribution history;
- simple monthly insights and warnings for pots forecast after their needed-by date.

Money Pot management and editing remain in the dedicated Money Pots workspace.
